# Product

Facts below come from the owner's brief relayed on Nov 8th, 2026 and from the repository. Nothing here was invented.

## Platform

web

## Users

Casual people who arrive from a Google search with one file to convert or fix: a PDF to merge or split, an iPhone photo to turn into JPG, an image to shrink, a CSV to open as a spreadsheet. They want it done in a minute and then they leave. Most are not technical and many are on a phone.

## Product Purpose

Stayfile (stayfile.app) is a set of free browser file tools for PDF, image (including HEIC to JPG) and CSV/JSON/Excel conversions. Success is a visitor dropping a file and saving the result without friction or doubt.

## Positioning

Every tool runs inside the browser tab. Files are never uploaded, there is no sign-up, and it is free. Competing converter sites upload files to a server.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- 15 tool pages across PDF, Images, and Spreadsheets and data; URLs, titles, meta and structured data must not change.
- Fully static and client-side (Astro). Cross-origin isolated (COOP/COEP), so fonts and scripts are same-origin.
- Ads come later: layouts must leave room for an empty ad slot without breaking.
- No scroll effects, 3D, heavy animation or large images.
- Copy rules in `.github/agent/daily.md`: plain specific sentences, no sales words, no dashes, never claim something the site does not do.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Stayfile. Green has been the brand color.
- The owner dislikes templated, text-heavy pages and wants the site to be visual.
- Must look clearly different from the sibling site SubFixer (subfixer.app: cinema-ticket theme, orange and black, heavy grotesk) and from generic converter sites.

## Evidence on Hand

No testimonials, user counts or press. Do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

- The tool is the page: the drop zone is the first thing a visitor sees.
- Show, do not tell: the privacy claim should be visible in the design, not argued in paragraphs.
- One file, one minute: nothing stands between dropping and saving.
